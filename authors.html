Original credited writer

D. Mongeni

Every recovered sidecar repeats D. Mongeni in the writer field, preserving the primary script credit across the original run. That establishes the documented writing role without pretending the archive can tell us who contributed each premise, scene, or joke.

The surviving archive does not include a fuller biography or a strip-by-strip division of writing duties, so this page does not manufacture one.

Writing contributor · Artist · Editor

Jake Herrmann

Jake contributed writing, jokes, dialogue, and story work alongside the surviving primary writing credit. He also drew and edited the comic under the Long Haired Syndicate Studios name—then returned years later to put the recovered run back online.

Yes, the artist named a main character Jake. No, this page will not be convening an ethics panel about it.
