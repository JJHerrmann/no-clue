The Core Four

LogicHaus, when dramatic assembly is required

Joe

Confirmed

Host · Inventor · Bad idea accelerator

  • Has the four-room apartment that becomes the group’s accidental refuge.
  • Created K1 through K9; an old joke in K9’s code made the ninth unit the “Champion of the AI Quickening.”
  • Builds things involving DNA, shrink rays, scanners, and consequences that usually reach kaiju scale.

Jake

Confirmed

Room two · Gas-station survivor · K9’s Master

  • Moves into Joe’s second room after a spectacularly bad breakup.
  • Works at Greedy Gary’s Gas, where workplace safety includes an infernal robber-disposal mechanism.
  • Acts as K9’s day-to-day authority and the household’s most persistent source of rules.

Confirmed

Classic K9 · Confirmed

K9

Origin recovered

Artificial intelligence · Holographic interface · Ninth of nine

K9 is the ninth K-series unit built by Joe. A forgotten “HighLvl.exe” joke caused him to eliminate K1–K8, leaving K9 the sole champion of an AI-themed Quickening.

He calls Jake “Master,” occasionally promotes Chris to “Chris-Master,” carries instant-message participants into holographic space, projects drawing interfaces, and has a documented tendency to blame the footstool.

Open archive questions

  • Several inserts and specials do not map cleanly to numbered episodes.
  • No file named 015 survives. The script does contain “Legacy Strip #15,” apparently paired with a later-numbered image, so file and episode numbering remain unresolved.
  • Some biographies are necessarily incomplete because the surviving run ends mid-monster fight.
  • The proper original K9 is the light-grey sphere with the red eye. The dark sphere and diamond verse-forms belong to the reconstructed site’s presentation.
